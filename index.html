    One word at a time, at a pace you set. The rate you ask for and the rate actually delivered are drawn as two marks on one scale — they should sit on top of each other. Most readers of this kind run 20–25% slower than the number they display. This one will not make you read faster; what it does is keep your place, fit almost no screen, and tell the truth about its own speed.
